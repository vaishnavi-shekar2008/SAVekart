Maths behind SAVEkart

01 · Warehouse objective
C(W) = Σ dᵢ × D(rᵢ, W)

Demand-weighted distance keeps high-order regions closer to the warehouse. SAVEkart uses the Haversine distance and weighted Lloyd's iterations already present in the optimizer.

02 · Carbon model
CO₂ = Σ km × deliveries × EF

The demo estimates emissions from delivery distance and an adjustable vehicle emission factor. Lower network distance therefore reduces estimated CO₂.

03 · Dynamic pricing
P = P₀(1 + αD + βT − γG)

Distance and peak demand increase the simulated delivery price; grouped “crowd-house” deliveries reduce it by sharing the last-mile trip.
